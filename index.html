About Me

Academic experience with a focus on student growth

I am a Lecturer and Head of the Computer Systems Department at Ararat Technical Institute in the Kurdistan Region of Iraq. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from the University of Duhok and a Master's degree in Information Technology from Duhok Polytechnic University.

My work brings together teaching, student mentoring, departmental coordination, and educational activities. I value helping students develop not only their academic knowledge but also communication, teamwork, and problem-solving skills they can use in their future careers.

I am particularly interested in student development, career-readiness training, professional skills, and practical learning experiences that connect education with the workplace.
